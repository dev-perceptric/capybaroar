# Blog plan

500 planned posts for `/blog/<slug>`, modeled on folk.app's blog (its full title list is in `docs/blog-plan-folk-reference.md`). 12 are already covered by live posts. The other 488 are split into 49 batches of 10.

## How to work it

- Take the next batch, write its posts, then set each row's status to `live`.
- Lists, alternatives and versus posts use the listicle build (`docs/blog-listicles.md`): every price and feature comes from captured vendor text in `site/build/listicles/text/`.
- "needs capture" means the tool is not in `site/build/listicles/tools.json` yet. Add it and run `node site/build/shots.mjs` before writing.
- Guides, reviews and tutorials need a post format the build does not have yet. Batch 29 is the first one that needs it.
- Years in titles follow the publish year. Planned titles say 2027.

## Running cost

$0 a month at Beta (50 teams) and Growth (500 teams). Posts are static pages; screenshots and covers render on a laptop.
