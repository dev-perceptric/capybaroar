# Rewrite and fit posts with AI

In the post editor, AI can rewrite your text, fit it to each network and suggest replies in the Inbox.

## Rewrite a post

1. **Open a post** in the calendar, or click **New post**.
2. **Write a first version**, even a rough one.
3. **Press Rewrite** in the text toolbar, or Ctrl+J, and keep the version you like.

## Fit to each network

One post rarely fits every network as is. X has 280 characters and LinkedIn has 3,000. Open a channel's tab and change the text for that network only, or ask AI to fit it. Each network's preview updates as you go.

## Suggested replies in the Inbox

In the **Inbox**, open a comment and press **Suggest reply**. Edit the draft and send it.

## What it costs

Each rewrite, fit, caption or suggested reply costs 4 [AI credits](https://capybaroar.com/docs/ai-credits). AI writing is on paid plans.
