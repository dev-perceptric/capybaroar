# Connect your first channel

A channel is one social account Capybaroar can post to, like your Instagram account or your LinkedIn Page.

## Steps

1. **Open Channels.** Click **Channels** in the sidebar.
2. **Pick a network.** Click the network you want to add.
3. **Sign in and approve.** Most networks open their own sign-in page. Sign in with the account you post from and approve Capybaroar. Bluesky and Discord ask for details in a short form instead.
4. **Check it's connected.** You land back on Channels, and the new channel shows as **Connected**. Post to it from the calendar.

## How each network connects

| Network | How you connect |

| [Instagram](https://capybaroar.com/docs/instagram), [Facebook](https://capybaroar.com/docs/facebook), [Threads](https://capybaroar.com/docs/threads) | Sign in with Meta |

| [TikTok](https://capybaroar.com/docs/tiktok) | Sign in with TikTok |

| [LinkedIn](https://capybaroar.com/docs/linkedin) | Sign in with LinkedIn, for your profile or a Page you manage |

| [YouTube](https://capybaroar.com/docs/youtube) | Sign in with the Google account that owns the channel |

| [X](https://capybaroar.com/docs/x), [Pinterest](https://capybaroar.com/docs/pinterest) | Sign in with that network |

| [Mastodon](https://capybaroar.com/docs/mastodon) | Type your server, then sign in there |

| [Bluesky](https://capybaroar.com/docs/bluesky) | Your handle and an app password |

| [Discord](https://capybaroar.com/docs/discord) | A webhook link for the channel |

## When a channel says Reconnect

Networks sign apps out from time to time, for example after a password change. Click **Reconnect** on the channel and sign in again. Scheduled posts stay in place.

## How many channels you can add

Free covers 3 channels, Creator 8, Pro 30 and Agency 100.
