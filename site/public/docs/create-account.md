# Create your account

Sign up with your email in under a minute. You start on 14 days of Pro, free, with no card.

## Steps

1. **Open the sign-up page.** Go to [app.capybaroar.com/signup](https://app.capybaroar.com/signup).
2. **Fill in the form.** Enter your name, company, email and a password, then press **Create account**.
3. **Follow the welcome steps.** The welcome screen walks you through two things: **Connect a channel** and **Schedule a post**.

## Your free trial

New workspaces get 14 days of Pro with 200 AI credits. You don't need a card. When the trial ends you move to the Free plan unless you pick a paid plan, and your posts and channels stay where they are.

## Inviting your team

Pro includes 5 members and Agency includes 15. Clients who only approve posts don't take a seat. Invite people from **Settings**.

Source: https://capybaroar.com/docs/create-account
