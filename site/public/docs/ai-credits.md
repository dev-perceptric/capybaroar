# AI credits

Everything AI in Capybaroar draws from one credit balance: the agent, writing help, pictures, videos and voiceovers.

## Credits on each plan

| Plan | AI credits a month |

| Free | – |

| Creator | 1,000 |

| Pro | 2,500 |

| Agency | 8,000 |

| Pro trial (14 days) | 200 |

Plan credits refill on your billing date each month, on yearly plans too. Unused ones don't carry over.

## What things cost

- **Rewrite, fit to a network, suggested reply or caption:** 4 credits.

- **Build a link in bio page with AI:** 50 credits.

- **An agent message:** what it really used, at least 2 credits. Long jobs, like a week of posts with pictures, use more.

- **Pictures and videos:** priced by the model and length. A short video costs the most.

1,000 credits make about 125 images, or about 6 weeks of posts planned by the agent. If something fails, you get its credits back.

## Buying more

On a paid plan you can buy packs: 1,000 credits for $12, 5,000 for $50 or 20,000 for $160. Bought credits last 12 months and are used after your plan credits. You can also turn on automatic top-up, which buys a pack when your balance gets low.

## Limits per client

On Agency you can cap how many credits each client brand uses a month, so one client can't use up the whole pool.
