# The AI agent

The agent is Claude with every Capybaroar action. Tell it what you need and it plans and writes posts, makes pictures and checks your results.

## What the agent can do

- Look at your channels and recent posts, then plan and write new ones.

- Save posts as drafts at your queue times, or schedule them when you ask.

- Make pictures and videos for posts and attach them when they're ready.

- Read your analytics and tell you which posts did best.

- Edit or delete posts. It asks before deleting.

It uses the same actions and checks you do, so it can't go past your plan's limits or post to a channel you can't use.

## Start a chat

1. **Open Agents.** Click **Agents** in the sidebar, then **New chat**.
2. **Say what you want.** For example: "Plan next week for our bakery launch: one LinkedIn post a day, Monday to Friday at 10 AM. Save them as drafts."
3. **Check the drafts.** The agent tells you what it saved. Open them in the calendar to edit, then schedule.

## Drafts first

The agent saves drafts unless you ask it to schedule. Nothing goes out until you, or the agent on your request, schedule it.

## What it costs

The agent is on paid plans and uses [AI credits](https://capybaroar.com/docs/ai-credits). Each message costs what it really used, with a small minimum. Pictures and videos it makes are charged on their own.
