# Plan a week of posts with the agent

One message, five days of drafts. Here is how to ask so the first try is close to right.

## Steps

1. **Open a new chat** in **Agents**.
2. **Give the topic and the goal.** "We're launching a sourdough class on the 20th. Get sign-ups."
3. **Name the channels and the rhythm.** "One LinkedIn post and one Instagram post a day, Monday to Friday."
4. **Say when.** "At my queue times" or "at 9 AM New York time".
5. **Say what to save.** "Save them as drafts." The agent saves drafts by default anyway.
6. **Review in the calendar.** Open each draft, tweak it, check the preview and schedule.

## Prompts to copy

- "Look at my channels and recent posts, then write a week of posts about [topic]. Save them as drafts at my next queue times."

- "Turn this article into a LinkedIn post and an X post, and save both as drafts: [link]"

- "Make a picture for each draft this week and attach it."

- "Show my results for the last 30 days and tell me which posts did best."

## Getting better results

- Paste a few of your best past posts so it can match your voice.

- Give facts it can't guess: prices, dates, names and links.

- Ask for changes in the same chat: "Make Wednesday's shorter and add a question."
