# Schedule your first post

Write a post once, check how it looks on each network, then schedule it, queue it or post it now.

## Steps

1. **Open the calendar.** Click **Calendar**, then **New post**.
2. **Pick channels.** Choose one or more connected channels at the top of the editor.
3. **Write your post.** Type your text. The counter shows how much room each network has left.
4. **Add pictures or a video.** Upload files or pick them from your media. Some networks need media, like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest.
5. **Set network options.** Click a channel's tab to change things for that network only, like an Instagram Reel, a YouTube title or a Pinterest board. You can also change the text for one network.
6. **Check the preview.** The right side shows how the post will look on each network. Click a video to play it.
7. **Choose when it goes out.**
**Schedule:** pick a date and time.
8. **Add to queue:** put it in your next open queue time.
9. **Post now:** publish right away.
10. **Save draft:** keep it for later.

## After it posts

Open the post again from the calendar to see each channel's result. When a network gives Capybaroar the post's address, an **Open post** link shows next to that channel. If a network turns a post down, its reason shows in the same place.

## Tips

- Use a first comment on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn for links or hashtags.

- Upload many posts at once with **Bulk** in the calendar.

- Let the [agent](https://capybaroar.com/docs/agent) write a whole week of drafts, then edit and schedule them here.
