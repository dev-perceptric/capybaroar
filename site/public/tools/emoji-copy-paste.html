How to copy and paste emoji

  1. Find your emoji. Scroll the categories, tap a category chip to jump to it, or type in the search box. Search knows names and common words, so "laugh", "love" or "party" all work.
  2. Click it. The emoji is copied right away and added to the box at the top.
  3. Paste it. Press Ctrl+V on Windows or Cmd+V on a Mac. On a phone, press and hold where you want it and tap Paste.

Want several? Click them one after another. They line up in the box, and Copy all copies the whole line. The ⌫ button removes the last one, and Clear empties the box.

Skin tones

Pick a skin tone from the six circles next to the search box. Every emoji that supports skin tones switches to that tone, like hands, faces and people at work. Your choice is remembered next time you visit. Emoji that only come in one color, like animals and objects, stay the same.

Using emoji in your social posts

  • Lead with words, not emoji. Put the emoji after your first few words so the opening still reads clearly in previews and notifications.
  • Use emoji as bullets. ✅, 👉 and 📍 make tidy list markers in long captions.
  • Watch the count on X. Each emoji counts as 2 characters there. Check long posts with our character counter.
  • Don't replace keywords. Search reads words, not pictures. Write "pizza 🍕", not just "🍕".
  • Think about screen readers. They read every emoji's name out loud. Five emoji in a row means five names read one after another.

Why emoji look different on other devices

Every emoji has an official name and code, but Apple, Google, Samsung and Microsoft each draw their own pictures. A 😂 on an iPhone and a 😂 on an Android phone mean the same thing, but they don't look exactly alike. That's why the emoji on this page look like the ones on your own device.

Windows shows country flags as two letters, like "US" or "GB", instead of a flag picture. Phones and Macs show the flags.

How do I copy an emoji?

How do I paste an emoji?

Press Ctrl+V on Windows or Cmd+V on a Mac. On a phone, press and hold where you want it, then tap Paste.

How do I change the skin tone?

Pick one of the six circles next to the search box. Every emoji that supports skin tones switches to that tone.

How many emoji are there?

This picker has all 1,923 emoji in Emoji 18, not counting skin tone versions. With skin tones, there are thousands more.

Why do I see a box instead of an emoji?

Your device doesn't have that emoji yet. Updating your phone or computer usually fixes it. People with newer devices still see it.

Why do I see letters instead of a flag?

Windows doesn't draw country flags as pictures, so it shows the two-letter country code. Phones and Macs show the flag.

Do emoji count toward character limits?

Yes. On X each emoji counts as 2. On other networks some emoji, like flags and families, count as several characters.
