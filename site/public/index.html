Questions. Answers.

Something else? Write to hello@capybaroar.com.

What is an agentic social media platform?

A social media tool where AI agents do part of the work. In Capybaroar, an agent can plan and write posts, suggest replies and write captions. You check its work before anything goes out.

Will the agent post without asking me?

It saves drafts unless you ask it to schedule. If a brand needs approval, posts wait for a yes from an owner, an admin or the client.

Which networks can I post to?

LinkedIn profiles and Pages, Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, X, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon and Discord.

Can I use Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor?

Yes. Every plan has an MCP server and a REST API, so Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT or your own code can plan and schedule posts. See the API docs.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Every new workspace gets 14 days of Pro. After that it moves to Free unless you upgrade.

What happens if I go over the Free limits?

Nothing you scheduled is lost. You can’t add more channels, or schedule more posts that month, until you upgrade or the month turns over.

Does it work for agencies?

Yes. Give each client a brand with its own channels, invite them as a client to approve posts, and send them reports. Agency adds your logo and color to reports.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from Settings, Billing. Your plan stays active to the end of the paid month.
