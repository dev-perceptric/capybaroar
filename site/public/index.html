FAQ

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Every new workspace gets 14 days of Pro. After that it moves to Free unless you upgrade.

What happens if I go over the Free limits?

Nothing you scheduled is lost. You can’t add more channels or posts that month until you upgrade or the month turns over.

Which networks can I post to?

LinkedIn profiles and Pages, Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, X, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord and Google Business.

Can AI agents schedule posts for me?

Yes. Every plan has an MCP server and a REST API, so Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT or your own code can plan and schedule posts. See the API page.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from Settings, Billing. Your plan stays active to the end of the paid month.
