Delete your data

To remove one channel, open Channels in the app and remove it. We delete its tokens right away.

To delete your whole account and everything in it (workspaces, channels, posts and media), email hello@capybaroar.com from your account email. We delete everything within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you connected a network through Facebook, Instagram or Threads, you can also remove Capybaroar from that network’s app settings. That stops our access at once.
