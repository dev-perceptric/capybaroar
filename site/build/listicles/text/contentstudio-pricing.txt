Which plan is right for me?
Standard ($19 per month billed annually, or $29 month to month) is for solo creators and freelancers managing one brand. Advanced ($49 per month billed annually, or $69 month to month) suits growing teams that need a social inbox, approval workflows, automation and competitor analytics across multiple workspaces. Agency Unlimited ($99 per month billed annually, or $139 month to month) is built for agencies, with unlimited users and workspaces and social accounts that scale affordably. Enterprise pricing is custom and adds bespoke limits, SSO, full API access, white-label branding and priority support. Not sure? Book a free 30 minute call and we'll help you pick.
Do you charge per user or per seat?
No. We don't multiply your whole plan price by every team member the way per seat tools do. Standard includes a single user and doesn't offer seat extras. Advanced includes a set number of users, with additional seats available as a low cost extra. Agency Unlimited and Enterprise include unlimited users. Your team can grow without your bill jumping a full tier for every person.
How much do I save with annual billing?
Paying yearly saves you up to 34% versus paying month to month. Standard drops from $29 to $19 per month, Advanced from $69 to $49, and Agency Unlimited from $139 to $99. You pay once for the year up front and lock in the lower rate.
Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Yes. You can move from monthly to annual at any time to capture the discount, and the change is reflected on your next charge.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
Yes. You can change plans whenever your needs change, right from your billing settings. Upgrades and extras are prorated within your current billing cycle, so you only pay for the difference. To downgrade, you can remove add-ons or move the whole plan down to a lower tier, and any remaining balance is refunded.
How does billing work and what payment methods do you accept?
Subscriptions are billed securely through Paddle, our payment provider, on a monthly or yearly cycle. We accept major credit and debit cards, and you can view and download every invoice from Settings → Billing & Plan. Any applicable tax or VAT is calculated automatically at checkout based on your location.
What counts as a social account?
Each connected profile counts as one, so a Facebook page, an Instagram profile and a LinkedIn page are three social accounts. Standard includes 5, Advanced includes 10, and Agency Unlimited starts at 25 with room to scale.
What happens when I reach my plan's limits?
Your work stays put. Nothing is deleted or cut off. On Advanced and Agency Unlimited you can add more capacity instantly, or move up a plan if that's a better fit.
Can I add more accounts, users or workspaces without changing plans?
Yes, on Advanced and Agency Unlimited (Standard doesn't include extras, so you'd upgrade to Advanced first). You can add capacity directly from billing settings, with pricing shown in real time before you confirm and prorated to your billing cycle. On the Agency Unlimited plan, users and workspaces are already unlimited, and extra social accounts use tiered pricing that gets cheaper as you scale.
Is ContentStudio a good fit for agencies managing multiple clients?
Yes, it's built for it. Agency Unlimited gives you unlimited workspaces (one clean dashboard per client), unlimited team members, white-label options, approval workflows and client management, with social accounts that get cheaper per account the more you add.
How does the free trial work? Do I need a credit card?
You get a one time 7-day free trial with full access to the entire ContentStudio suite, with no credit card required to start. Add payment details whenever you're ready, during or after the trial.
What happens when my trial ends?
If you haven't added payment details, nothing is charged. To keep going, just choose a plan and your workspaces, connected accounts and scheduled content carry straight over.
Is there a free forever plan?
No. ContentStudio doesn't offer a free forever plan or lifetime (LTD) deals. The only free option is the 7-day trial, after which a paid plan keeps your account active.
Can I pause my subscription?
Yes. If you need a break, you can pause billing instead of cancelling. Your data, workspaces and settings stay safe, and you can resume anytime with full access restored. Pausing takes effect immediately or at the end of your current billing cycle, your choice.
Can I cancel anytime, and do you offer refunds?
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your billing settings (cancellation is handled by the account's Super Admin). You keep access through the end of your paid period, and your data is retained. We also offer a 30 day money back guarantee for first time subscribers, starting from your initial paid subscription date. Contact support and we'll handle eligible refunds. You can find the full details in our Terms and Condition.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. We take data security seriously. ContentStudio is GDPR compliant, hosted on top tier European data centers with SSL encryption, and independently assessed through Google's CASA security program every year. Your data is managed securely and can be deleted on request.
Do you offer custom or enterprise plans?
Yes. Enterprise includes custom account limits, unlimited users and workspaces, SSO (SAML), full API access, white-label options and a dedicated account manager. Contact sales or book a demo for a tailored quote.
Can I purchase extra API credits as well as AI credits?
Yes. Additional API credits can be purchased on Annual plans, Agency Unlimited plans, and dedicated API plans. Additional AI credits can also be purchased separately based on your usage requirements.
Are AI credits for images different from AI credits for videos?
Yes. Image generation and video generation use different AI providers and models, each with its own credit consumption. Keeping them separate allows for more accurate usage tracking and billing.
Do you offer an affiliate program and how can I earn rewards?
Yes. You can earn a 30% recurring commission for the first year by referring ContentStudio to your network. Once your referral becomes a paying customer, you'll receive recurring commissions based on their subscription payments. You can find the full details in our Affiliate Program Terms and Conditions.
I cannot access the Unified Social Inbox. Why?
The Unified Social Inbox is not available on the Standard plan. To access this feature, you will need to upgrade to either the Advanced or Agency Unlimited plan.
Can I retain my account data if I don't pay for the subscription before the due date?
Yes. ContentStudio retains account data for up to 90 days after subscription expiration. After this period, the data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
What plan includes customer support? Is it an add-on?
Customer support is included with all ContentStudio plans at no additional cost. Enterprise customers may also receive access to dedicated support resources depending on their agreement.
Need an onboarding session for ContentStudio. How can I schedule one?
You can book an onboarding or consultation session through our website or by reaching out to our support team, and we'll be happy to assist.
