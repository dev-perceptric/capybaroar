How Tailwind grows your Pinterest
Find what works

Keyword Research surfaces the Pinterest keywords with the highest potential for your niche. It combines type-ahead suggestions, Trends data, and audience signals into a single Resonance Score so you can compare keywords at a glance instead of piecing together data from five different sources.

1
Create content around it

SmartPin turns those keywords into fresh, ready-to-publish Pins automatically every week. It pulls photos from your site, writes the title and description, and generates a new Pin design, so you get a steady stream of content without the manual creative work.

2
Schedule it

Your new Pins go out at the right times. SmartSchedule builds a publishing calendar with optimized time slots so the content SmartPin creates gets published consistently without you thinking about when to post.

3
Accelerate what's working

As your Pins go out and gain traction, Turbo puts the top performers in front of a community of thousands of professional Pinterest creators. Their genuine saves, comments, and clicks send engagement signals that help those Pins reach even more people on Pinterest, driving traffic back to your site.
