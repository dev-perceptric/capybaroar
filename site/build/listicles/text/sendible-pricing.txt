Frequently asked
questions.
What counts as a social profile?

Every social media account that you would like to connect to Sendible will count as one profile. For key networks like Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc., each social profile includes the ability to post, pull reports, and to stream comments and feeds. 

You can send 100 daily posts per user (or more depending on your plan) across all your social media profiles. 

How can I set up a calendar for a client, location or brand?

Every calendar lives inside a dedicated  workspace. Each workspace acts as an independent environment for your client, location, or brand, featuring its own content calendar, 6 social profiles, inbox, reports, and publishing queues.

If you need to manage multiple calendars across your account, you can easily add more workspaces or schedule a quick demo with our team to find the perfect setup.

Can I add additional profiles or workspaces to my plan?

Absolutely. You can easily scale your account's capacity without needing to upgrade your entire plan tier.

We bundle profiles and workspaces together: every additional workspace you add includes 6 extra social profiles. You can add or remove these bundles at any time directly within your subscription settings.

(Note: If you are looking for advanced features rather than just more volume, we recommend upgrading your base plan.)

 

Which social media networks do you support?

Sendible supports key social media networks including Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and Google My Business. You can also monitor and respond to comments on selected platforms and generate quick reports to analyse your efforts and make better strategic decisions in the future.

What Instagram functionality does Sendible have?

You can publish photos, videos, Stories and image carousels directly to Instagram Professional profiles. You can also schedule reminders for video carousel posts with in-app notifications.

You can also tag profiles in image posts, geotag locations and automatically include a first comment filled with your hashtags to go out with every post. Unfortunately, we don't support answering direct messages on Instagram at the moment.

Do you support Google My Business?

Yes, we do support scheduling posts with images to Google My Business so you can keep your locations updated! Reports are not available at the moment and will need to be accessed natively.

Does Sendible have TikTok scheduling?

You can publish videos directly to TikTok Business and Personal Accounts via Sendible. Pick a thumbnail from the video timeline and enable duets, stitches, and comments.

Are there limits to posting?

In order to comply with the posting policies of social media sites, we apply a daily posting limit for each user. During the trial period, the limit is set to 100 posts per day per user. Paid users on the Traction plan and above have a higher daily posting limit.

This limit is put in place to prevent social media accounts from being suspended which can happen if your posts are flagged as spam or are reported by social media users for other reasons.

What type of customer support is available on each plan?

Get support from real people every step of the way. All plans include access to our helpdesk's how-to articles, live group training sessions, and our live chat which is available during UK business hours (9 am - 10 pm GMT).

1:1 onboarding and additional support are provided by our customer success team for customers on the Premium, Elite, and Enterprise plans.

What are my payment options?

We accept all major credit cards. Payments are usually taken monthly but we also offer a 15% discount if you choose to pay annually. If you would prefer to pay by invoice or purchase order, please contact us.

Please note: If you are a UK-based customer, you will also be billed VAT.

How does the White Label plan work?

If you're looking to generate more business as an agency, our White Label plan could be an excellent choice as it allows you to add all your clients into one branded space. 

You can also create different service packages for clients and limit certain areas of the dashboard until you upsell them to a higher tier with more hands-on work from you.

White Labelling is available on our Elite and Enterprise plans for an additional fee. Both plans include all Sendible features, with pricing starting at $349/month (USD) for Elite and $800/month (USD) for Enterprise. If you're interested, book a demo with our team to discuss this potential solution in detail.

Which currencies do you support?

We currently support three currencies: USD (U.S. Dollar), EUR (Euro), and GBP (British Pound). Unfortunately, we do not support any other currencies at the moment.

Are there contracts or commitments?

There are no contracts on our standard plans and you can cancel at any time. At the end of your free 14-day trial, you’ll be asked to add your credit card. If you add a credit card during the trial, you'll be billed automatically at the end of your trial period.

If you sign a contract for a custom or a White Label plan, the terms outlined in the contract will be applicable.

Do you offer discounts to nonprofits and charities?

Yes, we do! If you're a nonprofit or charitable organization, you can receive a monthly discount of 15% on any plan. If you choose to pay annually, you can get a 25% discount on any plan.

Please note that only one type of discount can be applied to your account at a time.
