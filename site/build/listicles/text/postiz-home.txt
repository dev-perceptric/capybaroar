Problem:
I record a video each week, but I only post it to YT and sometimes X.

I should be sharing it across every social media channel, but it's too much work.

Each platform has its own format and limitations:

- YT is video only.
- X is text + video.
- LinkedIn is text + max 15-min video.
- An article on dev .to is long-form and needs screenshots.

And I write differently on X and LinkedIn.

Solution:
My eve agent adapts the content to each platform and then publishes a draft using Postiz unified API.

The agent takes the YouTube video as input and generates posts for each platform in the required format.

This includes trimming the video, writing the copy, and generating screenshots.

The agent has access to my voice on each platform so it writes like me and doesn't sound like AI slop.

Everything it creates is a draft, and it's one click for me to publish.

I'm more careful with what I post on LinkedIn and X, so I don't let it autopost (yet), but having done 80% of the work for me is helpful regardless. I would never have the energy to cut a video down to 15 mins myself, for example.
