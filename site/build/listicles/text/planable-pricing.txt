Pricing FAQs
What is a workspace in Planable?

A workspace is where your team creates, reviews, approves, and schedules content for a specific brand or client. It keeps everything for that brand in one place, including social media pages, campaigns, collaborators, and shared assets.

Workspaces let you separate clients and brands, keep collaboration private, and customize workflows so each client relationship runs the way you need.
