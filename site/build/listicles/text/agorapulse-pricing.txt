Let’s Get Your Questions Answered
1. What happens after the trial?

You’ll pick a paid plan if you’re ready, or keep using the free plan for as long as you need. No pressure, no automatic charges.

2. Can I change plans later on?

Definitely. You can switch plans anytime.

3. Do I need to enter a credit card to start?

Nope! Just sign up and start your 30-day trial, no card required.

4. What’s the difference between a user and a profile?

A user is someone on your team. A profile is one of your connected social accounts.

5. What if I just want a quick look first?

We’ve got you. Just book a personalized demo, let us know what’s important to you, and we’ll show you how it works. No pressure, no commitments.

6. Do you offer special pricing for nonprofits?

We do. Reach out and we’ll help you get set up with the right plan at the right price.

7. Do all plans receive fast support?

Yes, every plan includes access to our award-winning support team. Most questions get answered in under an hour by real humans via chat, email, or live training.
