How does the 14-day free trial work?
You get full access to all features included in your chosen plan for 14 days. No credit card is required to sign up. You can connect your social profiles, schedule posts, test AI tools, and invite team members immediately. At the end of the trial, you can choose to subscribe or switch plans with zero commitment.
Can I bring my whole team without extra fees?
Yes! Unlike Hootsuite, Agorapulse or Sprout Social, which charge hefty per-seat fees for every team member, Sendible includes unlimited users on all of our plans. You can onboard your entire internal team and invite clients without unexpected monthly bills.
How much does Sendible cost per month?

Sendible offers 5 flexible pricing plans billed monthly (with a 15% discount available on annual billing). Unlimited users are included on all plans:

Core: $35/mo (1 workspace, 6 social profiles) – Ideal for 1 or 2 brands or locations.
Plus: $99/mo (3 workspaces, 18 social profiles) – Built for growing brands needing team collaboration.
Premium: $199/mo (7 workspaces, 42 social profiles) – Our most popular tier for scaling agencies needing custom branded reports and approval workflows.
Elite: $299/mo (15 workspaces, 90 social profiles) – Advanced productivity, dynamic fields, and permissions for large teams.
Enterprise: $750/mo (50 workspaces, 300 social profiles) – Dedicated account manager, SSO, and custom integrations.

All plans start with a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Can we lock down client access and separate brand workspaces?
Absolutely. Sendible’s architecture allows you to separate client brands, campaigns, or internal teams into Workspaces. You can set granular permission controls so clients only see their own accounts, approval queues, and reports, keeping data secure and organised.
Are there contracts or cancellation fees?
No contracts, no commitment, and zero cancellation fees. All Sendible plans are billed month-to-month (or annually for a 15% discount), and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly from your account settings.
How easily can we migrate our profiles and schedules from Hootsuite or Sprout Social?
 Migration takes just a few minutes. You can bulk-import your scheduled posts using a simple CSV file and reconnect your social accounts in a few clicks. Plus, our support team and step-by-step guides are available to help ensure zero downtime for your social posting. If you need more clarity, you can schedule a call with one of our consultants. 
Does Sendible offer a White Label social media management option?
Yes! Sendible offers a complete White Label solution as an add-on for our Elite and Enterprise plans. You can fully customise the platform with your agency's domain, logo, brand colours, and custom email notifications, giving your clients a seamless, fully branded portal to approve posts and view performance reports.
