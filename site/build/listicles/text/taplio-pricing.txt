Frequently Asked Questions
What is Taplio?

Taplio is the only all-in-one AI-powered tool to grow your brand on LinkedIn. Create viral posts, schedule them, engage with your network, and track results, all in minutes a day.

What do I get in the free trial?

The 7-day free trial gives you every Pro feature, with limited AI usage to get a taste. Your plan starts at the end of the 7 days, or as soon as you hit the AI limit.

What is the Taplio Chrome extension?

A free add-on that brings Taplio into LinkedIn: instant stats, your best posts, trending content, and quick saves. Works without a Taplio plan, but supercharges your experience.

Is Taplio safe?

Yes. Taplio is safe to use because it behaves exactly like a real LinkedIn user would: writing posts, commenting, and engaging naturally on your behalf. It doesn’t rely on hacks or shortcuts that put your account at risk. Everything is designed to respect LinkedIn’s normal usage patterns, so you can grow your presence with peace of mind.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you may cancel anytime. All you need to do is head over to the Settings section of Taplio and access the Billing tab. From there you'll be able to upgrade, downgrade or cancel your subscription.
