Frequently asked questions
What is Agorapulse?

Agorapulse is a social media management platform. Your team uses it to publish posts, answer comments and messages, track what people say about your brand, report on results, and measure the revenue from social. It works with Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, YouTube, and TikTok, among other networks.

What does Agorapulse AI do?

Agorapulse AI handles the repetitive parts of social media work. It suggests replies to direct messages, tags the sentiment of comments and mentions, translates messages, filters off-topic mentions out of listening searches, and explains your reports in plain language. Writing Assistant helps you draft and polish captions. Your team reviews each AI suggestion before anything goes out. Some features depend on your plan.

How is Agorapulse different from other social media management tools?

Agorapulse is built to show what social media is worth to your business. It connects to Google Analytics to track the traffic, leads, and revenue your posts and conversations drive, with no separate analytics add-on. Its inbox sorts comments, messages, and mentions with moderation rules, and it flags when a teammate is already replying. Listening covers social networks, news sites, blogs, and forums. On some plans, clients can approve posts without needing their own seat. G2 reviewers also rate Agorapulse highly for ease of use and quality of support.

What social networks does Agorapulse support?

Agorapulse works with Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Reddit, and Google Business Profile. Not every feature is available on every network, because each network sets its own rules for outside tools.
